WARNING SIGNS

Things worth checking carefully

Upfront Payments

Requests for registration fees, training fees, deposits, or other payments before work begins.

Sensitive Information

Requests for passwords, PINs, OTPs, or other sensitive information.

Pressure to Act

Urgent deadlines, limited slots, or pressure to pay or apply immediately.

Unrealistic Earnings

Claims of unusually high or guaranteed income for little or no experience.

Messaging Apps

Recruitment that relies heavily on WhatsApp, Telegram, or other difficult-to-verify channels.

Recruitment Schemes

Opportunities where earning depends mainly on recruiting other people.
